FAQ

Good questions.

Is there a minimum order?

No minimum. We print from just 1 piece, and larger orders get a lower price per piece.

Can I pay in dollars?

Yes. We can invoice in USD or JPY, and payment details come with your quote.

I don't have a finished design. Can you help?

Yes. Send us a sketch or idea and we'll create print-ready artwork for a small design fee.

Can I use my unit's official emblem?

Please confirm you have permission to use official insignia before ordering. We're happy to help design an original unit logo instead.

I need it fast. Can you rush?

Often, yes. Enter your event date in the quote builder and we'll confirm what's possible.
