FAQ

Do you have a minimum order?

No minimum. We print from just 1 piece, and larger orders get a lower price per piece.

Can you print our unit or ship logo?

Yes. Send us your logo, crest or a hand sketch (a phone photo is fine) and our designer turns it into print-ready artwork at no extra charge. Please make sure your command allows the use of the emblem.

Can every shirt have a different name or number?

Yes. We can print individual names, ranks or numbers on each shirt.

How fast can you make them?

Production is about 7 days after the design and order are confirmed. If you have a fixed date such as a homecoming, change of command or ball, tell us when you request a quote.

Can you quote in US dollars?

Yes. Our quote builder shows estimates in USD or JPY, and we send a final quote after checking your design.

Do you deliver to the base?

We are a local shop in Yokosuka and ship finished orders anywhere in Japan.
